# The game's main chapters

## 🎯 The big picture

| Stage | Chapter(s) | Big question | Decision areas in focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 🛰️ Exploration | Flyby test, Robots go first | "What's out there?" | ☀️ 📡 ⚡ |
| ⛺ Establishing a presence | First landing, Building the outpost | "Can we visit and set up?" | 🫁 ☀️ ⚡ |
| 🛡️ Survival | Survival | "Can we stay safely?" | ⚡ 🫁 ☀️ |
| 🌱 Expansion | Expansion | "Can we grow?" | 🌱 ⚡ 📡 |

Each chapter gives players only 2–3 things to think about, so it's never too confusing. By the end of the whole game, they will have tried the jobs a real Moon base needs.
